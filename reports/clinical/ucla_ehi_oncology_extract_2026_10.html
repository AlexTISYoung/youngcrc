Scope and interpretation

  • Selected oncology extracts from the supplied UCLA electronic health information export; this is not a complete medical record or a release of the original export.
  • Medication records cover selected intravenous anticancer drugs. A recorded New Bag dose and Stopped event do not independently establish the delivered total dose or complete treatment-course coverage.
  • Clinical history, proposed care and primary administration records retain separate evidence labels. Treatments delivered elsewhere may be described without original administration records.
  • Repeated historical text is summarized by encounter, not counted as a new treatment or laboratory observation. No new discrete laboratory measurements are inferred from note text or order metadata.
  • Existing primary imaging, pathology and laboratory results remain separate. Dates in later histories do not silently replace dates in primary reports.
