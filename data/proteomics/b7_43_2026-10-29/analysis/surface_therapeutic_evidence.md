# Surface-target therapeutic evidence — 29 October 2026

Scope: CDH17, PTK7, LRRC15 and B7-H3/CD276. This is a primary-source update to the 22 October analysis, independent of the new quantitative-proteomics calculations. Registry snapshots were retrieved directly from the ClinicalTrials.gov API on 29 October and saved in `registry/`. Recruitment describes the public record, not a confirmed available slot or individual eligibility. No trial centre was contacted.

## Main change since 22 October

**ARR-217/MRG007 now has actual CRC clinical response evidence.** The prior statement that its initial clinical efficacy was awaited is outdated. The sponsor's 25 October ESMO release reports, at the August 5 cutoff, 123 treated patients including 113 with CRC. At 6 mg/kg, 13/29 evaluable CRC patients had reported responses (44.8%), comprising **7 confirmed, 4 pending confirmation and 2 unconfirmed** responses. Thus 44.8% must not be described as confirmed ORR; 7/29 (24.1%) were confirmed at that cutoff. Across doses ≥4 mg/kg, 25/73 responses included 15 confirmed. Grade ≥3 treatment-related events occurred in 37/123 (30.1%), including neutropenia 12.2% and diarrhea 8.9%; no grade-5 treatment-related event or ILD was reported. Dose optimisation at 5 and 6 mg/kg is underway. These are uncontrolled, immature sponsor-reported conference data, not a comparison against AMT-676 or standard therapy. [Sponsor primary release](https://ir.arrivent.com/news-releases/news-release-details/arrivent-announces-initial-phase-1-data-cdh17-targeted-adc-arr).

## CDH17

**Clinical meaning:** supports consideration of CDH17 ADCs, now with early clinical evidence for two agents. Protein detection or a cohort-relative quantitative rank is not a validated response predictor or demonstration of accessible membrane antigen on present-day metastatic tumour. B7 is a treated rectal primary containing neighbouring bowel structures. Confirm malignant-cell membrane localisation, intensity, percentage positive and heterogeneity on current metastatic tissue if available, using the trial's central assay where required. Normal intestinal expression means tumour specificity cannot be inferred from bulk detection.

- **AMT-676, NCT06400485:** recruiting; registry last update 18 May 2026. Requires tumour tissue, measurable disease, ECOG 0–1 and standard-treatment exhaustion/intolerance. Excludes prior treatment to the same target or a TOP1-payload ADC; **ordinary irinotecan is not this exclusion**. Australian sites are listed recruiting. Do not claim verified UK access; country/city inconsistencies in nominal US registry entries require site confirmation. [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06400485).
- **Clinical evidence:** the ESMO GI 2026 abstract reports 23/125 responses (18.4%) among evaluable CRC patients; 94.4% of CRC patients had received irinotecan. Median PFS was 6.0 months in the 26-patient 6.4 mg/kg cohort. Response-confirmation status was not specified. Grade-3 anemia, neutropenia and diarrhea were 11.6%, 10.1% and 4.0%; one grade-3 colitis DLT occurred. This is preliminary single-arm evidence, not an individual benefit estimate. [Primary abstract](https://www.annalsofoncology.org/article/S0923-7534%2826%2900297-8/fulltext).
- **ARR-217/MRG007, NCT07066657:** recruiting; last update 13 August 2026. Public criteria require a CDH17-testable specimen or baseline biopsy, RECIST-measurable disease, ECOG 0–1 and lack of suitable standard therapy. Prior CDH17 therapy excludes; public text does not impose a blanket prior TOP1-ADC prohibition, but additional protocol exclusions apply. Numerous US sites are listed recruiting, including UCSF, Colorado, MD Anderson, Fred Hutchinson and Sarah Cannon centres. Initial CRC results now support this as a data-bearing alternative to AMT-676, without asserting superiority. [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07066657).
- **DB-1324, NCT07263594:** recruiting; last update 22 May 2026. No human CRC response result verified in this targeted update. Prior CDH17 therapy and TOP1 ADCs exclude. Recent clinically significant GI bleeding, obstruction, perforation or fistula are specific protocol concerns. [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07263594).
- **CHM2101 CAR-T, NCT06055439:** active, not recruiting, last update 22 October 2026. Do not present as an available enrolling alternative. [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06055439).

All the above CDH17 approaches remain investigational; neither detection nor China breakthrough designation establishes an approved CRC indication.

## PTK7

**KIVU-107, NCT07229313:** recruiting phase I; last update 24 September 2026. Broad refractory-solid-tumour criteria and CRC in the registry keywords provide a credible CRC screening lead, but do not establish an open accepting cohort. Public criteria exclude prior PTK7 therapy or any TOP1-payload ADC; they do not state a blanket conventional-irinotecan exclusion. ECOG 0–1 and measurable disease required. US and Australian sites listed recruiting. No human CRC response outcomes were verified. [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07229313), [primary trial-in-progress abstract](https://ascopubs.org/doi/pdfdirect/10.1200/JCO.2026.44.16_suppl.TPS3158).

**IDE034, NCT07503808:** recruiting phase I; last update 18 June 2026. B7-H3×PTK7 TOP1 ADC with CRC explicitly included. Archived tissue for expression testing required. **Any TOP1 inhibitor within six months**, including conventional irinotecan, excludes under the current public criteria. Prior B7-H3 or PTK7 ADC also excludes. Recent irinotecan therefore remains a major gate. [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07503808).

The sponsor designs IDE034 to internalise when both antigens coexist on the same cell and anticipates first clinical data at the end of 2026/early 2027. Bulk detection of PTK7 and B7-H3 in the same tissue cannot establish same-cell coexpression. Use tumour-membrane IHC and, if pursuing IDE034, spatial/multiplex coexpression assessment rather than adding separate bulk ranks. [Sponsor clinical update](https://ir.ideayabio.com/2026-08-04-IDEAYA-Biosciences-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results-and-Provides-Business-Update).

PTK7 remains an investigational lead rather than a biomarker for an approved treatment. The original report's DAY301, LY4175408 and HWK007 matches should not substitute for CRC-specific cohort checks; the October 22 registry audit found their listed histologies did not include CRC.

## LRRC15

**ZL-6201, NCT07374848:** recruiting phase Ia/b; last update 23 July 2026. The NYU primary page explicitly includes colon cancer among selected epithelial tumours. This is an early exploratory route, with no human CRC efficacy verified. [NYU study page](https://clinicaltrials.med.nyu.edu/cancer/clinicaltrial/3066/an-open-label-phase-1ab/?qd=77054292).

**An important eligibility qualification:** the registry limits selected epithelial-solid-tumour patients to **no more than three previous systemic lines in the metastatic/relapsed refractory setting**. Prior TOP1 ADC excludes, whereas ordinary irinotecan is not explicitly prohibited by that clause. Tissue, ECOG 0–1 and measurable disease are required. [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07374848).

LRRC15 IHC should separately score cancer-associated stroma and malignant cells. A high bulk rank does not identify cellular source or imply direct malignant-cell targeting. The sponsor's preclinical poster reports LRRC15-positive fibroblasts in 24/28 colon-cancer samples and models of stromal delivery/bystander killing, which supports the rationale but is not a patient's predictive test. [Primary preclinical poster](https://www.zailaboratory.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2026_ZL-6201-AACR-Poster.pdf). The sponsor's October investor material still anticipates proof-of-concept data in the first half of 2027. [Primary investor disclosure](https://ir.zailaboratory.com/node/15316/ixbrl-viewer).

## B7-H3 / CD276

**Risvutatug rezetecan, NCT06885034 / EMBOLD PanGI-201:** recruiting, last registry update 19 October 2026; Manchester is listed recruiting. CRC is explicitly included. Prior TOP1 ADC excludes; conventional irinotecan is not the same exposure. Clinically significant bleeding in the preceding month and current/prior relevant pneumonitis are material concerns. [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06885034), [GSK study page](https://www.gsk-studyregister.com/trials/223675).

The ASCO GI protocol abstract and hospital listing describe **one to two previous CRC treatment lines**. The abbreviated latest registry text does not repeat that limit, so report it as an important protocol gate needing confirmation, not proof that it has been removed. [Primary protocol abstract](https://ascopubs.org/doi/pdfdirect/10.1200/JCO.2026.44.2_suppl.TPS264), [hospital/network listing](https://recherchesantequebec.ca/public/projects/455901).

The wider **EMBOLD PanTumor-101, NCT06551142**, is recruiting; last registry update 22 May 2026. It has broad phase-I monotherapy criteria after standard therapies and a three-prior-line limit for combination escalation. CRC-specific available cohorts cannot be assumed. [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06551142). GSK's October ESMO releases concern small-cell lung cancer and osteosarcoma; those outcomes must not be represented as CRC efficacy. [GSK ESMO programme](https://www.gsk.com/en-gb/media/press-releases/gsk-showcases-pipeline-momentum-across-lung-gynaecological-and-gastrointestinal-cancers-at-esmo-2026/).

**Ifinatamab deruxtecan, NCT06330064:** recruiting; updated 26 October 2026. Its CRC cohort still explicitly requires **no previous irinotecan/topotecan or other TOP1 inhibitor**, in addition to a restrictive prior-line framework. Thus it remains unsuitable under the published CRC criteria for a patient with prior irinotecan. [Registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06330064), [NCI primary listing](https://www.cancer.gov/research/participate/clinical-trials-search/v?id=NCI-2025-00431).

**Interpretation:** the previous report showed CD276 detection but low relative rank, so it should not be described as B7-H3-high. Tumour-cell membrane IHC and heterogeneity matter; an aggregate intensity or abundance measurement does not establish membrane density, same-cell PTK7 coexpression or response. B7-H3-targeted CRC approaches remain investigational.

## Implications for an updated therapeutic catalogue

1. Upgrade ARR-217/MRG007 from efficacy-awaited to early CRC activity, retaining confirmation counts and toxicity limitations.
2. Preserve CDH17 and PTK7 as practical target-validation leads; neither assay provides a validated response threshold.
3. Retain LRRC15 as exploratory, explicitly adding its three-prior-line limit and the need for compartment-specific staining.
4. Preserve the irinotecan distinctions: it is not a universal TOP1-ADC exclusion, but is specifically relevant to IDE034's six-month interval and ifinatamab's CRC prohibition.
5. Treat GSK's prior-line restriction and actual cohort availability as unresolved access gates; avoid importing lung or sarcoma efficacy into CRC.
6. Do not assert a new approved treatment indication for any of these four targets.
