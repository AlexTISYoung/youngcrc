# CD73/NT5E and additional stromal targets — 29 October 2026

**CD73 supports an adenosine-pathway hypothesis, not a treatment-selection result.** Thirteen unique peptides and the approximately +19 Astral rank shift do not measure enzyme activity, extracellular adenosine, or dependence of current tumour immunity on this pathway. Compartment-resolved CD73 assessment could be an exploratory addition if tissue permits, but no validated CRC response threshold follows from this proteomics result.

**Direct CRC evidence is limited.** Oleclumab plus durvalumab produced one complete response among 42 MSS/pMMR CRC expansion patients (2.4%), with a six-month PFS rate of 5.4%. Patients already had ≥10% CD73-positive tumour cells by a validated study IHC assay; assay positivity therefore did not reliably identify responders. [Primary phase-I publication](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00262-023-03430-6).

In first-line MSS CRC, randomised COLUMBIA-1 added oleclumab/durvalumab to FOLFOX/bevacizumab: ORR was 61.5% versus 46.2%, but median PFS was **10.9 versus 11.1 months**, with no significant PFS/OS benefit. The ≥50%-positive CD73 subgroup had an exploratory response difference without corresponding PFS separation. This does not establish reversal of checkpoint resistance, particularly after BOT/BAL. [Primary COLUMBIA-1 publication](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41416-024-02796-3).

**Quemliclustat must be distinguished from etrumadenant.** The published ARC-8 quemliclustat results concern pancreatic cancer; they cannot supply a CRC response estimate. [Primary ARC-8 publication](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13099643/). ARC-9 did include a quemliclustat/AB680 + etrumadenant + zimberelimab arm, but the study is completed and I did not verify published outcomes for that arm. [Current registry](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04660812).

The positive randomised **ARC-9 Cohort B** result instead used **etrumadenant, an A2A/A2B receptor antagonist**, plus zimberelimab/FOLFOX/bevacizumab. Versus regorafenib, PFS was 6.2 versus 2.1 months, OS 19.7 versus 9.5 months and confirmed ORR 17% versus 3%. Grade ≥3 events were 82% versus 49%. This supports continued investigation of the adenosine axis, but the multiagent comparison cannot isolate etrumadenant's contribution or validate bulk CD73 expression as a predictor. Prior checkpoint treatment was excluded, and the cohort was third-line. [Primary ARC-9 publication](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13320197/).

**ITGA5 and ENG remain lower-priority hypotheses.** PF-04605412 against α5β1 produced no objective responses in 33 advanced-solid-tumour patients and development stopped because of infusion reactions and inadequate target exposure/activity. [Primary trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4209234/). Endoglin/TRC105 plus bevacizumab did produce one CRC partial response in a small phase-I mixed-tumour trial; therefore “no human CRC signal” would be inaccurate. But that isolated uncontrolled response does not establish an expression-selected strategy or a current accessible programme. [Primary TRC105 study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4570619/). Bulk ITGA5/ENG elevation can reflect stromal/vascular composition and should not itself promote either target to an actionable therapeutic match.
